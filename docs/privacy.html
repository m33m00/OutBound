OUTBOUND

Privacy Policy

Updated November 6, 2026

OutBound helps airline employees plan standby travel. This policy says what OutBound collects, why, who helps run it, and how to delete it. OutBound has no ads, doesn't track you across other apps or websites, and never sells your information.

What we collect

Your account: your work email address, an account ID and your password. The password is stored only as a secure hash; nobody can read it.

What you share: seat counts you report and loads you ask about, with the flight and the time. Other people see the numbers, never who sent them; your account is linked to them behind the scenes so abuse can be stopped.

Searches and flight lookups: the airports, dates and flight numbers you look up, so OutBound can fetch schedules, flight status, airport delays and weather.

Location, only if you turn it on: used on your phone to find your nearest airport. For drive times, your location is rounded to about 100 meters and sent through our server to Apple Maps. It isn't stored.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, homes, saved routes and logbook stay on your phone.

Boarding pass scans and screenshots are read on your phone. The picture never leaves it; only the flight details you save go into your logbook on the phone.

Who helps run OutBound

Supabase hosts OutBound's accounts, database and server functions.

Apple Maps works out drive times from your rounded location.

AeroDataBox provides flight status for the flight numbers you look up.

Logo.dev shows airline logos; your phone asks it for a logo by the airline's web address.

The FAA and NOAA provide airport delays, weather and radar from public data.

Each of these gets only what it needs to do its job and is expected to protect it at least as well as we do.

How long we keep it

Your account and what you shared are kept until you delete your account.

Seat reports stop showing after the flight leaves, and are deleted with your account.

Server logs that may include your IP address are kept for a short time to keep the service running and safe.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Profile, then Account, then Delete Account. Your account, your email and everything you shared are deleted from our servers right away, and OutBound clears what it kept on your phone.

You can also turn off location in Settings or in your iPhone's Settings at any time.

Age

OutBound is for airline employees who are 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
