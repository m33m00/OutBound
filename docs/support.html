Support

Updated November 6, 2026

OutBound helps airline employees plan standby travel. It isn’t affiliated with any airline.

Get help

Open an issue at github.com/m33m00/OutBound/issues with what happened and your phone model.

Report a problem with a seat report

In the app, open the flight, tap the report and choose Flag Report. We review flags within 24 hours.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile, then Account, then Delete Account. Your account and everything you shared are deleted right away.
